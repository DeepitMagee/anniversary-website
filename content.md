# Page copy — every text field on the site

Edit anything you like in this file, then tell me and I'll apply it to the page and republish.

Rules of the file (all three matter):

- Each field starts with a `[key]` on its own line. The words below it are the text that appears on the page. Leave the keys alone so I can map your edits back.
- A `|` inside a value means "start a new line". Used in the big serif headlines.
- `*words*` in asterisks come out pink italic — that's the styling the current copy uses at the end of headlines.

Anything you change stays as you wrote it; anything you leave alone stays as it is. Blank space between fields is just for readability.
